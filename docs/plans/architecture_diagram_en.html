Multi-level supervision: Spatial Feature Maps, Soft Logits, and Localization Uncertainty
ℒtotal = ℒtask + 1.0 · ℒFGD + 1.0 · ℒcls(τ=2) + 1.0 · ℒloc(τ=10)
  • ℒtask (Ground Truth Segmentation Loss): Standard Ultralytics multi-task loss against reviewed annotations (Box IoU, Class BCE, Distribution Focal Loss, and Mask prototype/coefficients).
  • ℒFGD (Focal & Global Feature Distillation): Partitions Neck P3, P4, P5 features into Foreground (automotive components, α=1.0) and Background (engine bay chassis, α=0.5), aligning spatial attention and global cross-region relations.
  • ℒcls (Soft Logit KL Divergence): Mimics teacher class posterior distribution with temperature τ = 2.0 and background discount weight 0.05, capturing dark knowledge across visually similar components.
  • ℒloc (Localization Distillation): Distills the 4-boundary bounding box distribution (DFL) with temperature τ = 10.0, teaching the student teacher boundary uncertainty under partial occlusion.
